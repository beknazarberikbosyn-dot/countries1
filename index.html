    About the data

    The atlas follows a loop through all ten countries that have been part of the Commonwealth of Independent States: the eight full members, Turkmenistan as an associate member, and Moldova, whose withdrawal takes effect in 2027.

    Figures were checked in November 2026. Population comes from each country’s latest official estimate or census. GDP and GDP per person are IMF projections for 2026, in current US dollars.

    Maps use Natural Earth outlines with internationally recognised borders, so Crimea is drawn as part of Ukraine. Each head of state appears as an embossed medallion rather than a photograph.
